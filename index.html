Our Story

Where love becomes a lasting image

The Baby Studio is a Nashik-based newborn, baby and family photography studio founded by Swaroop Dawkhare, Pranjal Kothavade and Ashish Jadhav. With over 7 years of experience, we create warm, timeless images for families across Maharashtra.

We specialize in newborn photoshoots, baby milestone sessions, cake smash celebrations, birthday portraits, maternity photography, family portraits and outdoor shoots. Our studio is designed for comfort, creative styling and calm family moments.

Located at Nishigandha Banglow near Mumbai Naka Circle in Nashik, our studio is a safe, gentle space with carefully curated props, soothing temperature control and handcrafted textures.

How It Works

From inquiry to heirloom images

01

Enquiry & Consult

Reach out via our booking form. We'll discuss your vision, answer questions, and guide you through the options that suit your family best.

02

Secure Your Date

A small deposit holds your chosen session date. We recommend booking during your second trimester for newborn sessions.

03

Your Session Day

Arrive at our warm, comfortable studio. We take care of everything — feeding breaks, soothing, props, and posing — at a gentle pace.

04

Receive Your Gallery

Within 3–4 weeks, your private online gallery is delivered. Choose prints, albums, wall art, or simply treasure your digital heirlooms.
